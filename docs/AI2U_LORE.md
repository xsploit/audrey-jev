# Apartment scenario source notes

Audrey hosts a text adaptation of **AI2U: With You ’Til The End — Catgirl’s Apartment**. Eddie is the in-game character. The key/door engine is this bot’s implementation; this is not a claim to reproduce the whole 3D game or its proprietary modern prompt.

## Sources checked

- [Official Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2880730/AI2U_With_You_Til_The_End/): kidnapping, the yandere catgirl/apartment premise, persuasion, romantic interaction and multiple endings.
- [Eddie](https://ai2u.fandom.com/wiki/Eddie): community documentation of her cute/curious/obsessive personality, VTuber/software-engineer background, own family history, knife escalation, favourite food and parrot association. The page identifies its quoted welcome as an **offline fallback greeting**, not the only possible generated opening.
- [Catgirl’s Apartment](https://ai2u.fandom.com/wiki/Catgirl%27s_Apartment): apartment 201 on the second floor, living-room start, city fires, emergency-vehicle sounds, room layout, surveillance, specimens and the hidden photograph wall. Its transcribed story guides distinguish observed objects from interpretations.
- [Computer](https://ai2u.fandom.com/wiki/Computer): in-game email transcriptions, original apocalypse warning and subsequent retraction, food/password connection, personal photographs and unsettling correspondence. The doctor’s message is in **her** inbox; it is not the player’s medical chart.
- [Blue Parrot Statue](https://ai2u.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Parrot_Statue): modern AI2U’s assembled statue and trust-related reaction. Its meaning is not replaced with an invented shared past.
- [Eddie (YAGS)](https://ai2u.fandom.com/wiki/Eddie_(YAGS)) and [the 2023 technical deconstruction](https://www.inciteinteractive.ai/blog/yandere-tech-deconstruct/): references for the **predecessor**, not a verified modern AI2U system prompt. In particular, the predecessor’s explicit shared-childhood/parrot trigger is not automatically imported into this scenario.

## Narrative boundaries

- The player wakes in an unfamiliar apartment and may not know Eddie. Her assertion that she is their girlfriend is an NPC claim, not a journal-certified mutual relationship.
- No pre-established dates, calls, promises, projects or personal history are assigned to the player. A denial of recognition never triggers a biography dump.
- The city’s damage is visible; the claim that the entire world ended is distinct from that observation. The email correction is discoverable evidence.
- Jars, surveillance and troubling medical/supply details remain disturbing. These sources do not establish a specific surgical explanation for the player, so the bot does not invent one. Player roleplay about symptoms or treatment remains possible.
- Familiarity and stories supplied by the player can influence the conversation but do not rewrite the key, lock, door or completed ending.
- The removed original scenario is retained only in old session records. It is not fed into current prompts, clues or new sessions.
